Privacy Policy

Check Calo Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Check Calo collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Check Calo mobile application.

Last updated: April 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, Check Calo may collect the following information:

  • Account information such as your user ID, email address, and authentication session details.
  • Food entry data such as meal names, calories, macros, quantities, notes, timestamps, and related nutrition records.
  • Images captured by you through the in-app camera for meal logging and calorie estimation.
  • Barcode scan results when you use the in-app scanner.
  • App preference data such as reminder settings, theme preferences, and language settings.
  • Device-related identifiers used to support syncing and app functionality.

2. Permissions We Request

Check Calo may request the following permissions:

  • Camera: used to capture food photos and scan barcodes for nutrition tracking features.
  • Notifications: used to send optional calorie reminder notifications if you enable reminders in the app.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide meal logging, calorie estimation, and nutrition tracking features.
  • Save your food entries and preferences locally on your device.
  • Sync eligible account and food-entry data with backend services when backend configuration is enabled.
  • Upload food-entry images to configured cloud storage when syncing is enabled for authenticated users.
  • Send reminder notifications when you choose to enable them.
  • Maintain app security, authentication, and core product functionality.

4. Storage and Processing

  • Some app data is stored locally on your device for app functionality and offline use.
  • If backend services are configured, certain account data, food-entry records, and food images may be stored using Supabase services configured by the app publisher.
  • Authentication session data may be stored on-device to keep you signed in.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, including backend infrastructure and cloud storage providers, solely for providing app features.

6. Food Photos and Barcode Data

Food photos and barcode results are used to support meal entry, nutrition lookup, and calorie estimation. Food photos you choose to capture may remain on your device and may also be uploaded to configured cloud storage when syncing features are used by authenticated users.

7. Notifications

If you enable reminders, Check Calo may schedule local notifications on your device for meal or calorie reminders. You can disable these reminders at any time in the app settings or in your device notification settings.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide app functionality, maintain your account, support syncing, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Locally stored data may remain on your device until you remove it, uninstall the app, or clear app data. Cloud-stored data may remain until deleted by the app publisher or through supported account/data deletion processes.

9. Your Choices

  • You may decline camera or notification permissions, although some app features may not work correctly.
  • You may manage reminder settings in the app.
  • You may uninstall the app to stop future data collection from the device.

10. Children’s Privacy

Check Calo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted at the same URL with a revised “Last updated” date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data practices related to Check Calo, please use the developer contact information provided on the app’s Google Play listing or official support channel.
